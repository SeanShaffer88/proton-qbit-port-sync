Before you install

1. Install qBittorrent 5.x and the Proton VPN Windows app.
2. Enable port forwarding in your paid Proton VPN account.
3. In qBittorrent > Tools > Options > Advanced, select the Proton VPN network interface.
4. Exit qBittorrent completely, including its tray icon.

Setup runs for your Windows account without administrator rights. It installs the background watcher, configures its password-protected local API, and starts it automatically at sign-in. No script commands are required.

Existing Port Sync installations are upgraded while preserving settings and credentials. If you already use a separate qBittorrent Web UI, use the ZIP installer to supply its current credentials.

The uninstaller stops the watcher and removes startup. It retains your runtime settings, backup, and encrypted credential in LocalAppData\ProtonQbitPortSync, and leaves your qBittorrent settings in place.
